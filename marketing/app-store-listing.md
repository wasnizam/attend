## Why it is set up this way (ASO in short)

- **Search weight on the App Store:** app name (strongest) → subtitle → hidden keyword field (100 characters). A word counts once: never repeat a word across these three fields.
- **Primary language is English (U.K.)**, not English (U.S.). English (U.K.) is the language the App Store searches in Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Brunei, India, the Philippines, the UK, Czechia and most other countries.
- **Malaysia searches both English (U.K.) and Malay.** The Malay listing is extra keyword space in Malaysia, so its keywords are different words, not translations of the English ones.
- **Extra listings:** Malay, English (U.S.) for America, Czech for Czechia, Indonesian for Indonesia. Each one adds its own name, subtitle and 100 keyword characters in those countries.
- **Screenshot captions are searched too** (since 2025): every caption below carries search words.
- **After launch, ratings and how often people keep using the app matter more than the words.** Ask happy lecturers for a rating after their first good week (Dobry already asks for feedback; add the App Store rating prompt once the app is live).

**Description:**

One simple app for every kind of attendance: lecturers taking class attendance, trainers checking in course participants, and employers clocking staff in and out. Show a QR code, everyone scans it with their own phone camera, and the list fills itself.

WHAT MAKES DOBRY DIFFERENT

• Nothing to install for the people checking in
Students, course participants and staff use the camera they already have. No app, no account, no download. Only you need Dobry.

• No machine to buy
No fingerprint scanner, card reader or time clock on the wall. Any phone, tablet or laptop screen becomes your attendance point: in the lecture hall, at the training room door or at the office entrance.

• Hard to cheat
The QR code changes every 45 seconds, so a photo or link sent to someone else stops working. One phone counts for one person, and an optional location check makes sure people are really there. No proxy attendance in class, no buddy punching at work.

• It does the paperwork, not just the list
Most attendance tools stop at a list of names. Dobry finishes the job:
– Lecturers: the 80% rule, students at risk, warning letters and the barring list
– Trainers: attendance certificates and check-in records for training claims
– Employers: lateness, overtime, leave and payroll-ready hours

• Three uses, one app
Choose classes, training or workplace when you start, and Dobry shows the screens and words that fit.

• Public holidays handled for you
Holidays for your country and state are added every year, so no class, course or shift is wrongly marked absent.

• Ready in a minute, free to start
Add a class, course or shift, press start, show the code. No setup project, no IT department.

## For App Review

- **Sign-in for the reviewer:** create a demo account yourself (a lecturer account with one class and a few sessions), and put its email and password in App Review Information. Do not use your own account.
- **Notes:**

  Dobry is an attendance service for lecturers, trainers and workplaces. Please sign in with the demo account in Sign-In Information.

  To try check-in: on the Today screen, open a class and tap Start Attendance; a QR code appears. On a second device, scan it with the camera, or open Dobry and tap "Scan QR to check in" on the sign-in screen. The name appears on the first device's live list.

  Built for the device: a built-in QR scanner using the camera, Sign in with Apple and Google, reports and certificates saved or shared through the iOS share sheet, haptic feedback on check-in, a screen for when there is no connection, an onboarding screen, iPad layouts and dark mode.

  Account deletion: Account → Delete account.

- **Sign in with Apple** is offered next to Google, as guideline 4.8 requires.
- **Account deletion** is in the app (guideline 5.1.1(v)).
- **Watch out for guideline 4.2 (minimum functionality):** Apple sometimes rejects apps that only show a website. Dobry has native sign-in (Apple, Google), the phone's status bar and haptics, and will have push notifications; mention these in the notes if asked.
