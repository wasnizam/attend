# Dobry: App Store and Google Play listing

App Store Connect app: **Dobry: Smart QR Attendance** (Apple ID 6833051828, bundle `app.dobry`, SKU `DOBRY-IOS-001`).

## Why it is set up this way (ASO in short)

- **Search weight on the App Store:** app name (strongest) → subtitle → hidden keyword field (100 characters). A word counts once: never repeat a word across these three fields.
- **Primary language is English (U.K.)**, not English (U.S.). English (U.K.) is the language the App Store searches in Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Brunei, India, the Philippines, the UK, Czechia and most other countries.
- **Malaysia searches both English (U.K.) and Malay.** The Malay listing is extra keyword space in Malaysia, so its keywords are different words, not translations of the English ones.
- **Extra listings:** Malay, English (U.S.) for America, Czech for Czechia, Indonesian for Indonesia. Each one adds its own name, subtitle and 100 keyword characters in those countries.
- **Screenshot captions are searched too** (since 2025): every caption below carries search words.
- **After launch, ratings and how often people keep using the app matter more than the words.** Ask happy lecturers for a rating after their first good week (Dobry already asks for feedback; add the App Store rating prompt once the app is live).

## For App Review

- **Sign-in for the reviewer:** create a demo account yourself (a lecturer account with one class and a few sessions), and put its email and password in App Review Information. Do not use your own account.
- **Notes:** "Dobry is an attendance service. The app opens the same service as dobry.app. To try check-in, start a session in the demo account and scan the QR code shown on screen with another device, or open the check-in link shown under the code. Account deletion: Account → Delete account."
- **Sign in with Apple** is offered next to Google, as guideline 4.8 requires.
- **Account deletion** is in the app (guideline 5.1.1(v)).
- **Watch out for guideline 4.2 (minimum functionality):** Apple sometimes rejects apps that only show a website. Dobry has native sign-in (Apple, Google), the phone's status bar and haptics, and will have push notifications; mention these in the notes if asked.
